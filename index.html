      About Road Dash

      Road Dash is a free online car dodging game you can play instantly in your browser — no download, no install, and no account needed. Steer between three lanes, dodge oncoming traffic, and see how far you can get before you crash. It works on desktop and mobile, and your best score is saved automatically on your device.

      How to play

      On desktop, use the ← and → arrow keys (or A and D) to switch lanes. On mobile, swipe left or right on the road, tap the left or right side of the road, or use the on-screen arrow buttons. Collect gold coins for bonus points and a growing combo multiplier, and grab the blue shield power-up to smash through one crash for free. The road gets faster the longer you survive, so timing your lane changes matters more the longer your run goes.

      Features

      • Three difficulty levels — Easy, Normal, and Hard
      • Daily play streaks with a starting-shield bonus for returning players
      • Unlockable car color skins, bought with coins earned in-game
      • 10 achievements to unlock as you play
      • A local top-5 leaderboard and day/night visual themes
      • Install it as an app and keep playing offline
      • Share a score card to WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook and challenge your friends
      • Fully responsive — plays the same on phones, tablets, and desktops

      Frequently asked questions

      Is Road Dash free to play?

      Yes. Road Dash is completely free, with no purchases required to play or to progress.

      Do I need to create an account?

      No. Your best score, unlocked skins, and achievements are saved locally in your browser — there's no sign-up or login.

      Can I play Road Dash on my phone?

      Yes. The game is fully responsive and supports touch controls (swipe, tap the sides of the road, or use the on-screen buttons) on any phone or tablet.

      Can I install Road Dash as an app?

      Yes. Tap the Install app button to add Road Dash to your home screen or desktop. It launches full screen and keeps working offline.

      How do I share my score with friends?

      After a run, tap Share to send a ready-made score card to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and more — your friends get a link to challenge your score.
